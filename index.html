FAQ
How does FreshHop work? +
You place a pickup order on the store's own website and pay the store. Then you text us the pickup details. We collect a flat $9.99 delivery fee through a secure payment link, pick up your order, and deliver it to you.
How do I pay? +
Two separate payments: you pay the store directly for your items (on their website), and you pay FreshHop's $9.99 delivery fee through our secure Stripe payment link before we pick up.
Which stores do you deliver from? +
Any store that offers online pickup ordering — grocery stores, big-box retailers, restaurants with pickup, and more. If you can place a pickup order, we can deliver it.
How fast is delivery? +
As soon as your order is ready for pickup and your delivery fee is paid, we head to the store. Most deliveries arrive within the store's pickup window.
Which areas do you serve? +
We're starting local. Text us your delivery address and we'll confirm right away whether we can reach you.
What if the store cancels or delays my order? +
Since you paid the store directly, any refund comes from them. If your order can't be picked up, just let us know — you won't be charged a delivery fee for a trip we didn't make.
